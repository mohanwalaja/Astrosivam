## 6. What is deliberately NOT in this list

Stated plainly so the gaps are visible rather than papered over:

1. **No Gita Press Hindi jyotish book is listed.** The only catalogue I found for them
   (REF-04) is dead. Their print editions exist, but I could not open a verifiable page, so
   they are out until a working source is found.
2. **Uttara Kalamrita has no free full text (EN-09).** It is catalogue-verified only. Any
   rule attributed to it must come from someone holding the printed book.
3. **Ten temple articles are `linked-not-opened` (TP-04…TP-12).** The graha → temple →
   weekday mapping is safe because TP-01 was read, but temple-specific detail beyond that
   must not be quoted until the individual article is opened. **TP-03 (Thirunallar) is the
   exception — it was read in full and is the best temple source in the list.**
4. **Most Tamil books are metadata-verified, not page-read.** Their titles, language and
   collection are confirmed, so the citations are real. But **no verse has been read from
   them yet.** The knowledge base must therefore cite them at *book* level, not at
   *verse* level, until each is opened. TA-02 is the one Tamil book whose item page I read.
5. **No verse-level quote is made anywhere in this Part 1 table** except the Phaladeepika
   index anchors in §1, which were read directly.
6. **Fasting days, charity items and gemstone cautions are not yet sourced at rule level.**
   The books that will supply them are registered (TA-07, TA-10, HI-06, HI-07, HI-08) but
   not yet read. That is the first job of Part 2.
